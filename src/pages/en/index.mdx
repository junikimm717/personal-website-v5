I'm a junior at MIT majoring in Electrical Engineering with Computing
(6-5) with plans to double major in Mathematics (18). My interests are varied
and continue to expand, but mostly revolve around computer systems and the
infrastructure needed to make them more efficient and resilient. With the AI
boom going wild, I guess that means LLMs and GPUs are on the table :)

The last couple of years have moved me from compilers to distributed systems and
back. I spent most of last year as an undergraduate researcher in MIT's
[COMMIT](https://commit.csail.mit.edu/) group, trying to make sparse tensor computation
faster. Then came a 7-month stint as a platform engineer at
[Systalyze](https://systalyze.com) screaming at distributed system bugs and
experiencing the broken world of performance measurement. This
fall and spring, I am back at MIT CSAIL researching accurate LLM workload
profiling with Saman Amarasinghe, Manya Ghobadi, and Jeremy Carin. It is
my hope that I will better understand whether a systems PhD is right for me
after this experience.

Certain times of the year get dominated by a
new deranged [project](/en/dev) and my unstoppable obsession with completing it.
Most recently I got a
[statically linked Python](https://github.com/junikimm717/static-python)
running ~13% faster than the stock interpreter — something upstream explicitly
doesn't support — with
[public benchmarks](https://junikimm717.github.io/static-python) to back it up.
Before that I built [Mimux](https://github.com/junikimm717/lfs), a Linux
distribution bootstrapped from scratch and named after my late cat, then turned
the lessons into my 2-week IAP course, [6.S913](/en/6s913), where students built
their own functional Linux from scratch.

During my high school years, I was an olympiad participant hacking computers and
developing web apps on the side when my parents weren't watching :) In 2023, I
placed 23rd overall in the USAMO and attended [MOP](/en/mop), where I met many
of the friends with whom I have been attending MIT. Some side projects I had
been developing for the math contest community then unexpectedly turned into a
first job at [MathDash](https://mathdash.com) during my senior year of high
school.

Before all that (back to middle school!), I was a hardcore épée fencing main
with a dream to represent the US at the Cadet World Championships; in late 2019,
I reached first in my age group in the US. Alas, the COVID-19 pandemic hit, all
competitions and training came to a halt, and I found better and more fun things
to do with that time.
