I'm a junior at MIT majoring in Electrical Engineering with Computing
(6-5) with plans to double major in Mathematics (18). My interests are varied
and continue to expand, but mostly revolve around computer systems and the
infrastructure needed to make them more efficient and resilient. With the AI
boom going wild, I guess that means LLMs and GPUs are on the table :)

This fall and spring, I am researching accurate LLM workload profiling with
Saman Amarasinghe, Manya Ghobadi, and Jeremy Carin after a 7-month stint as a
platform engineer at [Systalyze](https://systalyze.com) taming workloads with
agents and crying at distributed system bugs. It is my hope that I will better
understand whether a systems PhD is right for me after this experience.

Certain times of the year get dominated by a new deranged [project](/en/dev) and
my unstoppable obsession with completing it. Most recently I used compiler
options and monkey patching to speed up
[Python](https://github.com/junikimm717/static-python) by >10% with [public
benchmarks](https://junikimm717.github.io/static-python) to back it up.
I also built [Mimux](https://github.com/junikimm717/lfs), my own
from-scratch Linux distribution, and turned
the lessons I gained from that into my 2-week IAP course, [6.S913](/en/6s913),
where students built their own mini Linux from scratch.

During my high school years, I was an Olympiad participant hacking computers and
developing web apps on the side when my parents weren't watching :) In 2023, I
placed 23rd overall in the USAMO and attended [MOP](/en/mop), where I met many
of the friends with whom I have been attending MIT. Some projects I had
developed for the contest math community then unexpectedly turned into a
first job at [MathDash](https://mathdash.com) during my senior year of high
school, at which point my career took a sharp turn toward startups.

Before all that (back to middle school!), I was a hardcore épée fencing main
with a dream to represent the US at the Cadet World Championships; in late 2019,
I reached first in my age group in the US. Alas, the COVID-19 pandemic hit, all
competitions and training came to a halt, and I found better and more fun things
to do with that time.
